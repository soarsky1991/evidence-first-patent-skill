### What to extract—and what not to say / 提取什么，不应声称什么

| Teaching field / 教学字段 | Safe record / 可安全记录 | Do not claim / 不应声称 |
|---|---|---|
| Bibliographic facts / 书目信息 | publication number, publication date, public URL, access date / 公开号、公开日、公开链接、访问日 | current validity, enforceability, or ownership / 当前有效性、可执行性或权属 |
| Expressly described components / 明示部件 | the component names and stated positional or coupling relations, with a source locator / 部件名称及明确写出的位置或连接关系，并附来源定位 | that the extracted list is complete, essential, or claim scope / 提取清单完整、必要，或等同于权利要求范围 |
| Family-related metadata / 家族相关元数据 | provider-labelled application or family identifier, plus provider and access date / 数据提供方标注的申请号或家族标识，以及提供方和访问日 | a legal family determination or date conclusion beyond the displayed record / 超出页面记录的法律家族认定或日期结论 |

### Reading workflow / 阅读步骤

1. Record the public URL, displayed publication number, publication date, provider, and access date.
2. Copy only a short source locator or paraphrase into an evidence record; keep component names and relations separate from claimed effects.
3. Mark any proposed comparison, redesign, or claimed benefit as `designed` until a suitable source or measurement record exists.
4. When the source page, evidence interpretation, or source locator changes, invalidate the affected extraction, comparison note, and downstream claim trace for human review.

1. 记录公开链接、页面显示的公开号和公开日、数据提供方及访问日。
2. 仅把简短来源定位或转述写入证据记录；将部件与关系同效果主张分开。
3. 任何拟议对比、再设计或收益主张，在具备合格来源或测量记录前，均标为 `designed`。
4. 如来源页面、证据解释或来源定位发生变化，应使受影响的提取记录、对比笔记和下游权项溯源失效，转交人工复核。

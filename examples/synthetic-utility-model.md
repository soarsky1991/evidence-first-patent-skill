## Structure / 结构

The product includes a housing, a guide groove in the housing, a retaining shoulder at the groove edge, and a removable cover engaging the shoulder. No method step is used as the sole subject of the independent claim.

该产品包括壳体、设置于壳体中的导向槽、位于导向槽边缘的保持肩，以及与保持肩配合的可拆盖。独立权利要求不以方法步骤作为唯一主题。

## Bilingual claim / 双语权利要求

1. A housing comprising a guide groove, a retaining shoulder at an edge of the guide groove, and a removable cover engaged with the retaining shoulder.
   一种壳体，包括导向槽、位于所述导向槽边缘的保持肩，以及与所述保持肩配合的可拆盖。

2. The housing of claim 1, wherein the removable cover is received in the guide groove.
   根据权利要求1所述的壳体，其中所述可拆盖容纳于所述导向槽中。
